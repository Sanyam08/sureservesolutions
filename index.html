How it works

Three steps. You can stop at any of them.

  1. 00:01

    I ask first

    I spot a moment, such as a launch date, a new market or a new product line, and ask whether the video side is already covered. Most of the time it is.

  2. 00:02

    We write a short brief

    If you want help, we pin down the scope, rough budget, timeline and style over email or a short call. Nothing goes to anyone until you say so.

  3. 00:03

    You meet one to three teams

    I introduce teams whose recent work and price range fit the brief. You talk to them directly and choose one, or none.
